Shiki 4.4.3, with its dependencies
==================================

The App Shell bundle carries Shiki and the packages below, which it depends
on. Dependencies it shares with react-markdown are listed in
react-markdown-remark-gfm-MIT-ISC.txt instead. Packages that share one licence
text are listed together above it; every text is reproduced as the package
ships it. The grammars and themes Shiki is given are in
Shiki-grammars-and-themes-NOTICE.txt.
